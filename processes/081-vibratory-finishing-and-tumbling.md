## Description

Parts are processed in rotating or vibrating media to deburr, clean, radius, or polish them.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Economical for batches of small parts. Offers limited control over specific surfaces and can damage delicate features.

## Examples

Fasteners, jewelry, cast fittings, stamped brackets.
