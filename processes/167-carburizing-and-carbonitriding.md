## Description

Carbon, or carbon plus nitrogen, diffuses into steel before hardening.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Creates a hard wear-resistant case over a tough core. Treatment is slow and may cause distortion.

## Examples

Gears, pins, camshafts, transmission components.
