## Description

X-rays or gamma rays create images based on material thickness and density.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Reveals internal voids, inclusions, assembly errors, and some cracks. Radiation control, access, interpretation, and poor sensitivity to some planar defects are drawbacks.

## Examples

Castings, welds, batteries, electronics, aerospace assemblies.
