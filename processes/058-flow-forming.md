## Description

Rollers compress a rotating cylindrical preform over a mandrel, reducing wall thickness and extending its length.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Creates strong, precise, thin-walled axisymmetric parts. Equipment is specialized and geometry is restricted.

## Examples

Rocket motor cases, pressure cylinders, wheels, drive shafts.
