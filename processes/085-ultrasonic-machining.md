## Description

A shaped tool vibrates ultrasonically while abrasive slurry erodes a brittle workpiece.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Machines hard, brittle, nonconductive materials with low thermal stress. Slow, and causes tool wear.

## Examples

Glass, sapphire, ceramics, carbide dies.
