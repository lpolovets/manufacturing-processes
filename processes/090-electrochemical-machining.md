## Description

Material is dissolved anodically using electrolyte while a shaped electrode approaches without touching.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Removes hard conductive materials rapidly without burrs, tool wear, or thermal damage. Electrolyte handling, localization, capital cost, and environmental controls are difficult.

## Examples

Turbine blades, blisks, fuel-system parts, aerospace passages.
