## Description

Fine metal powder mixed with binder is injection-molded, debound, and sintered.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Produces intricate small metal parts at scale. Tooling and development are expensive, sintering causes large shrinkage, and part size is limited.

## Examples

Surgical tools, watch parts, small gears, electronics hardware.
