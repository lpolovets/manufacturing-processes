## Description

Metal layers are compressed together through rolling, often with heat, to form a bonded laminate.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Efficiently combines corrosion resistance, conductivity, strength, and cost properties. Surface cleanliness, deformation compatibility, intermetallic growth, and downstream forming behavior must be controlled.

## Examples

Stainless-clad steel, copper-aluminum strip, cookware, battery materials.
