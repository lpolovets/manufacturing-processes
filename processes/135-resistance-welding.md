## Description

Electric current and clamping force locally fuse metal at the contact interface, without filler.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Requires no filler and is highly automatable. Mainly suits sheet, often needs two-sided access, and electrodes wear.

## Variants

### Spot welding

Overlapping sheets are clamped between electrodes and locally fused. Extremely fast and common in automotive assembly.

### Seam welding

Rotating wheel electrodes create overlapping welds along a seam. Useful for leak-resistant sheet-metal containers.

## Examples

Automotive body assembly, sheet-metal containers, appliances.
