## Description

Molten glass floats on molten tin, forming a flat sheet with highly uniform thickness and smooth surfaces.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The most efficient way to make large, optically smooth flat glass. Cannot directly produce shaped three-dimensional parts.

## Examples

Windows, mirrors, architectural glazing, automotive glass feedstock.

## Economic profile

Enormous continuous-process capex; high furnace-utilization dependence.
