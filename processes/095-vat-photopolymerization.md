## Description

Light selectively cures liquid photopolymer resin. Major variants include stereolithography and digital light processing.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Creates excellent detail and smooth surfaces. Resins may be brittle, UV-sensitive, temperature-limited, or poor representations of production polymers.

## Examples

Dental models, jewelry patterns, hearing-aid shells, microfluidic parts.
