## Description

Molten or softened particles are propelled onto a surface. Variants include plasma spray, flame spray, arc spray, and high-velocity oxygen-fuel spraying.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Applies thick wear-, heat-, or corrosion-resistant coatings without heating the full part. Bond strength, porosity, line-of-sight access, and surface preparation are critical.

## Examples

Turbine coatings, hydraulic rods, engine cylinders, repaired shafts.
