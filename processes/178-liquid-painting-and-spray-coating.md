## Description

Liquid coating is atomized or otherwise applied and then dried or cured.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Offers broad material, color, and substrate compatibility. Solvents, overspray, curing, surface preparation, and durability add complexity.

## Examples

Vehicles, aircraft, furniture, machinery.
