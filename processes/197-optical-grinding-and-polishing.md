## Description

Abrasives progressively generate and refine optical surfaces.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Supports many glass and crystal materials and can reach excellent figure and finish. Slow, and depends heavily on metrology and process stability.

## Examples

Lenses, telescope mirrors, prisms, windows.
