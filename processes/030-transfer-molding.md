## Description

Thermosetting material is forced from a chamber into a closed mold cavity and cured.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Handles inserts and more complex geometry than compression molding. Creates runner waste and requires more complex tooling.

## Examples

Encapsulated electronics, semiconductor packages, connectors, rubber parts.
