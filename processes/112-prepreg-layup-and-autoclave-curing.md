## Description

Pre-impregnated fiber sheets are placed in a mold, vacuum-bagged, and cured under heat and pressure.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Produces extremely high-quality composites with low void content. Materials, labor, cold storage, tooling, autoclaves, and cycle times are expensive.

## Examples

Aircraft wings, fuselage sections, satellites, racing structures.
