## Description

Steel is transformed, rapidly cooled to create hard martensite, and reheated to restore toughness.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Produces high strength and hardness with adjustable toughness. Quenching can cause distortion, cracking, and residual stress.

## Examples

Gears, tools, shafts, armor, fasteners.
