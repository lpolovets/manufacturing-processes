## Description

Parts or coupons are cut, loaded, sectioned, burned, fatigued, or otherwise destroyed to measure performance.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Provides direct evidence of strength, toughness, fatigue, bond integrity, microstructure, or failure mode. Consumes product and only samples a fraction of production.

## Examples

Tensile testing, fatigue testing, burst testing, metallography, peel testing.
