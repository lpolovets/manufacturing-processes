## Description

Cells rest and are measured for capacity, voltage retention, impedance, leakage, and self-discharge before sorting.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Identifies latent defects and allows cells to be matched into packs. Consumes time, floor space, equipment, and inventory capital.

## Examples

Cell binning and pack matching.
