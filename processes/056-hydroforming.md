## Description

Pressurized fluid forces sheet or tube into a die.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Creates complex seamless structures and can reduce part count. Equipment, tooling, pressure control, and material formability are demanding.

## Examples

Automotive subframes, exhaust components, bicycle frames, aerospace ducts.
