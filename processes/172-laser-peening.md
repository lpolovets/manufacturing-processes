## Description

High-energy laser pulses generate shock waves that create deep compressive stress.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Provides deeper fatigue enhancement than shot peening. Equipment and processing costs are high.

## Examples

Turbine blades, aircraft structures, nuclear components.
