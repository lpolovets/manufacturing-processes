## Description

Molten polymer is continuously forced through a die to produce a profile, pipe, sheet, film, coating, or compounded pellet. Major variants include profile extrusion, pipe extrusion, cast film, blown film, sheet extrusion, wire coating, and twin-screw compounding.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Among the highest-throughput and lowest-cost polymer processes. Geometry must remain substantially constant along the production direction, and quality depends on melt rheology, cooling, line stability, die swell, and feedstock consistency.

## Examples

Pipe, medical tubing, window profiles, wire insulation, plastic sheet, packaging film, weather seals, resin pellets.

## Economic profile

Moderate-to-high capex; excellent economics at scale; startup scrap and line utilization matter greatly.
