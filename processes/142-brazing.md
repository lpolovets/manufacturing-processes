## Description

Filler metal melts above roughly 450°C and flows into a close-fitting joint while the base materials remain solid.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Joins dissimilar materials, thin parts, and complex assemblies with limited distortion. Joint strength and high-temperature performance may be lower than welding, and gap control is critical.

## Examples

Heat exchangers, carbide tools, HVAC tubing, honeycomb structures.
