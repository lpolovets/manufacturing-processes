## Description

Controlled sparks erode conductive material through a dielectric gap.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Machines hard materials, delicate features, and sharp internal geometry without cutting forces. Only works on conductive materials and is relatively slow.

## Variants

### Wire EDM

Uses a continuously fed wire for through-cut profiles.

### Sinker EDM

Uses a shaped electrode to create blind cavities.

### Fast-hole EDM

Produces small, deep cooling or fluid holes.

### EDM drilling

Creates precise starter and high-aspect-ratio holes.

## Examples

Molds, dies, turbine cooling holes, punches, medical tools.
