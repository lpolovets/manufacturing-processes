## Description

A focused laser melts, burns, or vaporizes material along a programmed path.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Fast, precise, and highly automated for sheet material. Reflective metals, thick sections, capital cost, gas consumption, and heat-affected zones can be limiting.

## Examples

Enclosures, brackets, signage, gaskets, sheet-metal components.
