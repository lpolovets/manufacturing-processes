## Description

Sheet is held under tension and stretched around a die.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Creates large smooth curves with reduced wrinkling. Inefficient for small intricate parts and causes edge waste and thinning.

## Examples

Aircraft skins, railcar panels, architectural surfaces.
