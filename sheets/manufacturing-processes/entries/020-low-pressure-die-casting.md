## Description

Controlled gas pressure pushes molten metal into a reusable die.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Creates calmer filling and generally lower porosity than high-pressure die casting. Slower and less suited to extremely thin walls.

## Examples

Wheels, suspension components, cylinder heads, pressure housings.
