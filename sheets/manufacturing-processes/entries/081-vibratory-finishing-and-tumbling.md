## Description

Parts are processed in rotating or vibrating media to deburr, clean, radius, or polish them.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Economical for batches of small parts. Offers limited control over specific surfaces and can damage delicate features.

## When to use

Pick vibratory finishing or tumbling for bulk deburring, edge radiusing, cleaning, and pre-plate smoothing of batches of small robust parts — stampings, machined fittings, castings, fasteners — where cost per piece drops to cents and no operator touches individual parts. Cycle time in hours matters less than the near-zero labor. Avoid it for delicate thin walls, threads and sharp edges that must survive, parts that nest or dent each other, and anywhere a specific surface needs selective treatment; hand deburring, brush deburring, or abrasive-flow machining covers those cases.

## Examples

Fasteners, jewelry, cast fittings, stamped brackets.
