## Description

Production data is analyzed to detect variation, drift, and loss of process capability.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Identifies problems before they create widespread scrap and supports stable high-volume manufacturing. Only works when measurements are reliable and the underlying process is sufficiently understood.

## When to use

Apply SPC to repetitive production of measurable characteristics once the process is capable — as a rule, run control charts where Cpk exceeds about 1.33 so sampling can replace 100 percent inspection, and chart the few characteristics that actually predict function rather than everything measurable. It is standard practice, and often contractually required, in automotive and other high-volume supply chains. Prerequisites are non-negotiable: a gauge R&R showing measurement variation well under tolerance, and enough process understanding to act on out-of-control signals rather than tamper. It adds little in job shops with lot sizes of a handful, on immature processes still being debugged, or on safety-critical characteristics with marginal capability, where 100 percent inspection or in-process monitoring must carry the load instead.

## Examples

Machining dimensions, molding pressure, coating thickness, battery loading, semiconductor critical dimensions.
