## Description

An explosive-generated pressure wave drives metal into a die, often through water.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Can form enormous parts with relatively simple tooling. Safety, regulation, facility requirements, repeatability, and cycle time limit use.

## Examples

Large aerospace panels, vessel heads, ship structures.
