## Description

A toothed tool with progressively larger cutting edges passes through or across the workpiece.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Produces repeated profiles extremely quickly and accurately. Broaches are expensive and geometry is constrained by straight-line tool travel.

## Examples

Keyways, splines, turbine-disc slots, polygonal holes.
