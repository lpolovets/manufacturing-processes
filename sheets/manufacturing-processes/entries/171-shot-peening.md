## Description

Small spherical media bombard a surface and create compressive residual stress.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Improves fatigue and stress-corrosion resistance. Poor control can damage surfaces or alter dimensions.

## Examples

Springs, gears, turbine blades, landing gear.
