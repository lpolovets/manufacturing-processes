## Description

Liquid binder selectively joins powder particles layer by layer.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Fast, requires no separate support structures in the powder bed, and can process metal, ceramic, or sand. Green parts are fragile and sintering causes shrinkage and distortion.

## Examples

Sand molds and cores, metal components, ceramic parts.
