## Description

Chopped fiber and resin are sprayed into an open mold and compacted.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Faster than hand layup for large simple parts. Fiber control and mechanical properties are inferior to continuous-fiber methods.

## Examples

Bathtubs, boat hulls, shower stalls, RV panels.
