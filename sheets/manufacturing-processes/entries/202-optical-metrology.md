## Description

Cameras, structured light, interferometers, confocal sensors, or laser scanners measure dimensions and surfaces without contact.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Fast and suitable for soft, delicate, or densely featured parts. Reflectivity, transparency, line of sight, and calibration can limit performance.

## Examples

Electronics, molded plastic, additive parts, stamped components, optics.
