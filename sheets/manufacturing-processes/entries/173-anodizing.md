## Description

An electrochemical process grows a controlled oxide layer on aluminum, titanium, or similar metals.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Improves corrosion resistance, wear, paint adhesion, insulation, and appearance. Changes dimensions slightly and may reduce fatigue performance.

## Examples

Aerospace parts, enclosures, bicycles, architectural aluminum.
