## Description

A multi-edge tool removes a small amount from an existing hole to improve size and finish.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Produces accurate smooth holes efficiently. Cannot correct major location or straightness errors.

## Examples

Dowel holes, precision bushings, valve guides, bearing bores.
