## Description

Powder or wire is fed into a laser-, electron-beam-, plasma-, or arc-generated melt pool and deposited onto a substrate. Variants: laser powder DED, laser wire DED, electron-beam DED, plasma DED, and wire-arc additive manufacturing.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Repairs high-value parts, adds features, and builds large structures. Accuracy and finish are relatively poor, distortion can be substantial, and machining is usually required.

## Examples

Turbine repair, large aerospace structures, mold repair, added bosses and flanges.
