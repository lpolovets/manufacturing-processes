## Description
Valve-regulated lead-acid with the sulfuric acid immobilized in a silica gel rather than a glass mat. The gel restricts acid stratification and spreads current more evenly through the plate stack, giving gel batteries the best deep-cycle endurance and high-temperature tolerance in the lead family — at the cost of higher internal resistance that rules out high-current duty like engine starting.

## Strengths and weaknesses
Deepest cycling tolerance of any lead-acid construction; resists stratification and plate corrosion; performs comparatively well in hot climates; sealed and maintenance-free. Weaknesses: poor high-rate discharge; sensitive to charge voltage precision (a few tenths of a volt of overcharge damages the gel structure); higher cost than AGM; the same fundamental energy-density ceiling as all lead chemistry.

## Examples
Wheelchairs and mobility scooters (the classic gel market), solar home systems in Africa and South Asia, marine house banks, telecom sites in hot regions.

## Economic profile
A durable specialty within lead-acid, priced above AGM and defended by installed-base habits in mobility and off-grid solar. Steadily ceding the solar segment to LFP, whose cycle-life economics now dominate anywhere daily cycling occurs.
