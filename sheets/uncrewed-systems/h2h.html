Head-to-head: the options that actually compete

Uncrewed systems rarely compete across domains — an air vehicle and a submarine are not alternatives. Within a domain they compete hard, and usually on cost per effect rather than on capability. The rows below are the choices that come up repeatedly in real programs. Specialty classes — gliders, cargo aircraft, swarm munitions, ground robots — live in the explorer.
