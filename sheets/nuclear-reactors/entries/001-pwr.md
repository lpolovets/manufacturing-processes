## Description
The dominant reactor on earth: ~70% of the world's ~440 power reactors. Ordinary water at 155 bar serves as both coolant and moderator, kept liquid at ~325 °C and passing its heat to a separate secondary loop that raises steam — the two-loop separation keeps radioactivity out of the turbine. Descended directly from Rickover's naval program, the PWR won not on elegance but on being first, funded, and endlessly iterated.

## Strengths and weaknesses
Unmatched operating record (tens of thousands of reactor-years), a global supply chain, mature regulation, and 90%+ fleet capacity factors in the US. Weaknesses: 155-bar pressure demands massive forgings and containments; ~33% thermal efficiency and ~300 °C output foreclose industrial-heat markets; large cores need active safety systems layered in depth; and Western new-builds have become bywords for overrun.

## When to use
Default to the PWR for gigawatt-scale baseload on a large grid wherever a serial builder (Korea, China, or a committed national program) and state-grade balance sheets are available — it is the lowest-physics-risk, lowest-regulatory-risk path to nuclear at scale, and the right anchor technology for a first-time nuclear nation that wants a proven design with a functioning global fuel market. Look elsewhere for grids under ~10 GW, industrial heat above 300 °C, or any buyer who cannot carry a $10B+ decade-long project; there, light-water SMRs inherit the same physics at a financeable size and HTGRs own the heat market.

## Variants
### Gen III+ evolutionary designs
AP1000 (passive safety, Vogtle/China), EPR (Finland, Flamanville, Hinkley — the overrun trilogy), APR-1400 (Korea's on-budget export), Hualong One (China's serial workhorse).

## Examples
Vogtle 3&4 — first US new builds in 30 years, ~$35B for 2.2 GW; Barakah (UAE, four APR-1400s delivered near schedule); China connecting several Hualong Ones yearly; France's 56-reactor fleet.

## Economic profile
The same design costs $2.5–4k/kW in China and Korea and $12–15k/kW in recent US/EU FOAK builds — the gap is serial execution, not physics. Fuel is a functioning global LEU market (~$5–10/MWh contribution). The PWR is simultaneously the safest bet in nuclear and the technology whose Western cost disease created the entire SMR and advanced-reactor industry.
