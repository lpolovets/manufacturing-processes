## Description
Fusion by implosion: crush a peppercorn-sized D-T capsule with converging laser energy until it ignites, burning in nanoseconds before it flies apart — confinement by inertia alone. The National Ignition Facility made history in December 2022 with the first laboratory ignition (3.15 MJ out, 2.05 MJ laser in), repeated since with gains above 2. The catch: NIF's wall-plug lasers consumed ~300 MJ per shot and fire a few times a day; a power plant needs gain ~50–100 at ten shots per second, forever.

## Strengths and weaknesses
The only approach with demonstrated ignition; no magnets, no steady-state plasma to sustain; separable subsystems (laser, target, chamber) that iterate independently. Weaknesses: the gap from scientific to engineering gain is a factor of ~100 in laser efficiency and repetition rate; targets must cost cents, not the thousands of dollars of hand-built NIF capsules, at millions per year; final optics stare directly at fusion neutrons; and NIF itself is a weapons-stewardship facility whose geometry (indirect drive) is knowingly inefficient for energy.

## When to use
Back laser inertial fusion if you weight demonstrated ignition above all else and believe diode-pumped laser efficiency and cents-per-unit target manufacturing can each improve by the required factor of ~100 — those two cost curves, not plasma physics, are the entire bet. The defense overlap is a genuine de-risker: stockpile-stewardship revenue can carry ventures across the long gap to power. If you like the inertial physics but not the laser economics, pulsed-magnetic drivers (Pacific Fusion) run the same wager on capacitors at a tenth the driver cost; if you need power-plant engineering plausibility now, look to MTF or the tokamak camp.

## Examples
NIF (LLNL — ignition, repeatedly), Xcimer Energy (Denver, excimer lasers at 10× NIF energy for cheap direct drive), Focused Energy (Texas/Darmstadt, proton fast ignition), Germany's Marvel Fusion, historic Laser Mégajoule (France) and Omega (Rochester).

## Economic profile
Ignition transformed the field's credibility and fundraising overnight, but the commercial chain — diode-pumped lasers at ~10% efficiency, mass-produced targets, chamber survivability — is each its own venture-scale program. The near-term revenue reality is defense: inertial fusion expertise is stockpile-stewardship expertise, and several startups quietly monetize that overlap while the power plant recedes into the 2040s.
