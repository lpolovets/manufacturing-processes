Head-to-head: the usual short list

A handful of modalities account for most of the argument in a sensor-suite review. The ones below genuinely compete for the same slot and budget on a robot. The specialty and proprioceptive sensors (event cameras, FOG IMUs, encoders, GNSS/RTK) live in the explorer.

RGB cameraAny distance it can resolve; geometry only by inferenceNeeds light; glare, motion blur, dirty lenses; learned depth hallucinates off-distributionExtreme — the GPU is the real price$2–20The task turns on semantics: reading, recognizing, or feeding learned policies. Cameras go on nearly everything, so the real question is what you pair with them.

ToF / structured light0.2–10 m; dense per-pixel depthIndoors only — direct sunlight collapses it; dark, shiny, and transparent surfaces vanishModerate — the sensor does the work$100s; sub-mm industrial units $5–15kYou're doing indoor grasping or bin-picking at arm's reach. It's usually the first thing to try for indoor depth.
