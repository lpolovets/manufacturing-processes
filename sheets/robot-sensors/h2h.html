Head-to-head: the usual short list

A handful of modalities do most of the arguing in any sensor-suite review — the ones below genuinely compete for the same slot and budget on a robot. The specialty and proprioceptive sensors (event cameras, FOG IMUs, encoders, GNSS/RTK) live in the explorer.
