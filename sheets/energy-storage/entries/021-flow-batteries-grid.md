## Description
The electrochemical answer to duration decoupling: energy lives in electrolyte tanks, power in the stack, so hours are added by adding cheap liquid rather than cells. Vanadium systems dominate deployments (with China building at gigawatt-hour scale), while zinc-bromine, all-iron, and organic chemistries chase lower electrolyte cost. Unlimited cycle life at full depth of discharge makes flow the natural daily-deep-cycling machine. (Chemistry detail lives in the Battery Chemistries sheet.)

## Strengths and weaknesses
Tank-scaled duration, 15,000+ cycles without fade, non-flammable, and electrolyte that retains asset value (vanadium can be leased). Weaknesses: 65–80% round trip after pumping losses; stacks, membranes, and plumbing keep system $/kWh above lithium at short durations; power density is low, so footprint is building-scale; and Western vendors remain subscale while Chinese state-backed projects set the price benchmarks.

## When to use
Choose flow for 8–12 hour duty cycled deeply every day — solar-heavy grids with evening ramps, microgrids, fire-sensitive or indoor sites — where 15,000+ cycles without fade or augmentation and a 20+ year electrolyte life beat lithium's repowering math; lease the vanadium to neutralize price swings. Insist on fleet operating data (increasingly available from China's GWh-scale projects) before banking stack O&M assumptions. Avoid it below ~6 hours, where LFP wins outright, on footprint-constrained sites, and for multi-day firming — there iron-air's $20/kWh energy capacity is the better weapon.

## Examples
Dalian 100 MW/400 MWh VRFB and China's multi-GWh pipeline (Rongke, state grid tenders), Invinity, CellCube, ESS Inc (iron), Sumitomo Electric fleets in Japan.

## Economic profile
The crossover argument in miniature: flow loses to lithium below ~6 hours and wins on paper above ~8–10 as tank costs dominate — if stack costs and O&M behave. Vanadium price volatility can swing system cost 30%; electrolyte leasing is the financial engineering that tames it. Bankability is arriving via Chinese fleet data rather than Western projects.
