Head-to-head: the options that actually compete

Counter-drone systems are usually bought as a layered set rather than as alternatives, but within each layer the choices compete directly. The tables below cover the two decisions that come up in every architecture: what finds the drone, and what stops it. The wider electronic warfare entries — standoff jamming, self-protection, decoys — address a different problem and live in the explorer.
